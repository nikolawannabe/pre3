Pre-3 charges families less than it costs to run a class. Rent, teacher pay and insurance have all gone up, and
we make up the difference with fundraising, donations and grants. That support is also what pays for
tuition assistance, so no family has to miss out because of cost.

## Donate

Every gift helps, and Pre-3 is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit, so your donation may be tax-deductible.

## Come to a fundraiser

Fundraisers are also a great way for families to get to know each other outside of class. Look for us at:

- The **refreshment table at the North Olympic Discovery Marathon**
- **Restaurant takeovers**, where part of your bill supports Pre-3
- **Bake sales and candy sales**

Watch our [events](/events/) and [Facebook page](https://www.facebook.com/peninsulapre3) for the next one.

## Where your support goes

- **Tuition assistance** for families who need it.
- **Our classroom and playground:** grant support recently paid for new floors and a cleaner, safer sensory room.
  Next on our list are privacy screening along the playground fence, and outdoor lights so the evening class can
  play outside longer.
- **Our teachers:** a yearly professional development stipend, to recruit and keep great teachers.
- **Family nights**, so families can connect outside the classroom.
