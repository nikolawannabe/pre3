At Pre-3, every child comes to class with a parent, grandparent or caregiver. Our early childhood teacher plans
the morning; the grown-ups play alongside, and take turns helping in the classroom. Morning classes are two
hours and the evening class is 90 minutes. Each class meets once a week, ten times a term.

## Our classes

Ages are your child's age in September. Mixed-age classes let brothers and sisters come together, and little
ones learn a lot from watching the bigger kids. When a class is full, you can still register on Jovial to join
its waitlist.

### What we do

Music, art, social play, dramatic play, outdoor play, science and sensory play, and the occasional field trip.
Most of all, children practice being with other children: sharing, taking turns, and handling big feelings,
with their grown-up close by.

## Terms and dates

Pre-3 runs four terms a year. Parent education meets every week in Fall, Winter and Spring; in Summer the
classes run without a parent educator.

{{< term-dates >}}

## How to join

1. **Register on Jovial.** Pick a class that fits your child's age and your week. If it's full, you'll join the waitlist.
2. **Pay the registration fee and tuition.** If cost is a worry, apply for tuition assistance (below).
3. **Send in your forms** and watch the safety video before your first class.
4. **Come to class!** Your teacher will add you to your class's text message group.
