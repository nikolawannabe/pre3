A cooperative is owned and run by the people who use it. At Pre-3 that's you: every family helps make the
program happen, and gets a say in how it runs.

## What we ask of families

Along with bringing yourself and your child to class each week, every family:

- **Helps in the classroom** as the teacher's aide, taking turns with the other grown-ups.
- **Joins the parent education discussion** each week, and the family meetings.
- **Supports our fundraisers**, which keep tuition low and pay for tuition assistance.

## Parent education

While the children play, the grown-ups take turns stepping aside for a weekly discussion with a parent
educator. Topics include child development, everyday parenting challenges, and the
resources our community has for young families. Parent education runs in Fall, Winter and Spring; there is no
parent educator in Summer.

Parenting, especially for new parents, can feel isolating. Talking it through each week with others at the same
stage is one of the best parts of Pre-3, and many families make lasting friendships here.

{{< callout >}}
We're welcoming new parent educators soon. Parent education is simply about learning together; there are no
credits, grades or certificates.
{{< /callout >}}

## Join the board

Pre-3 is run by a volunteer board of enrolled families and interested community members. Board members keep
the co-op going: they handle enrollment and tuition, the budget, fundraising, and the classroom. Because
families age out when their children turn 3½, there are new openings every year, and community members help
carry the work from year to year. No experience needed. If you'd like to help,
[email us](mailto:peninsulaprethree@gmail.com).
