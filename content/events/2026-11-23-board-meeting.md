The Pre-3 board meets one Monday evening most months to plan classes, fundraisers and the budget. You don't
need to be on the board to come: every family is welcome to listen in, share ideas, or find a way to help.

We're looking for a Vice-Chair, Treasurer, Fundraising Chair and Members-at-Large. Training is provided. See
[Join the board]({{< relref "/families#join-the-board" >}}) for what each role does.
