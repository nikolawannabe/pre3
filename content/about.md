Peninsula Pre-3 Cooperative Preschool has been a first school for little ones in Port Angeles and the
surrounding area since 1975. Parents, grandparents and caregivers join their children each week for a morning
(or evening) of activities led by an experienced early childhood teacher, while the grown-ups learn together
too.

Pre-3 is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit, run by a volunteer board of enrolled families and interested community members.

## Our mission

At Pre-3, we work together to build a thriving, supportive community. We foster strong parenting and family
skills while giving young children a rich, engaging place to learn. We aim to set the standard for programs
serving families with children 10 months to 3½ years: affordable, meaningful, and open to every family who
wants to join us.

## Why it matters

For a young child, the world outside the family can be a lot. Sharing is hard, taking turns takes practice, and
little kids have big feelings. Pre-3 is a gentle place to learn to play with other children and to listen to
grown-ups outside the family, with a trusted adult right there.

For grown-ups, early parenting can be isolating, especially when one parent steps away from work to stay home.
Pre-3 gives parents a weekly community of people at the same stage, and practical tools for understanding and
responding to their child's behavior. Few programs on the Peninsula serve children this young, and fewer still
include the whole family.
