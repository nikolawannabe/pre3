Brand Colors

There were no set colors before this site, so these are new. They are soft pastels taken from the scene in the logo: water, forest, wildflowers and berries. Each color comes as a pair: the pastel for backgrounds, and a deep shade of the same color for text, icons and buttons. Never put text in a pastel color; it is too light to read.
